# 3D in a page: WebGL, Three.js and other canvases

A canvas is pixels: `gates.js` cannot read what it draws, and a model that looks fine on the
builder's laptop can be washed out, flat, hot and in the way of the scroll. Each rule below
has the check that proves it, read from the page with the renderer's own settings.

## Colour and light, set on purpose

Colour textures are sRGB and data maps (normal, roughness, metalness) linear; the output is
sRGB. A wrong space washes the colour out or crushes it, and nothing errors. Check: read
`renderer.outputColorSpace` and each texture's `colorSpace` in the page.
Tone mapping and exposure are chosen, not left at the default: a filmic or neutral curve for
lit products, none for flat illustration, and the exposure written down. Check: the two
values in the report, and the brightest surface not clipped to white in the capture.
Light has a key, a fill and a rim, or an environment map; one light from the camera makes a
cut-out. A contact shadow under the object puts it on a surface. Check: the capture shows
three tones on the object and a shadow where it rests.
(Colour spaces, tone mapping, three lights and the contact shadow: img2threejs, Apache-2.0.)

## Cost

The pixel ratio is capped at `Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`: a 3x phone otherwise renders
more than twice the pixels of 2x for no visible gain. Check: `renderer.getPixelRatio()` is 2
or less.
The loop stops when the canvas leaves the screen (an IntersectionObserver) and when the tab
is hidden (`visibilitychange`); an idle scene renders on change, not every frame. Check:
count frames for one second with the canvas scrolled out of view; the count is 0.

## People

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` stops auto-rotation and camera drift; the scene stays,
still. Check: the capture with reduced motion on matches itself a second later.
A still image of the scene is the first paint, swapped for the canvas once it renders, so
the page is not blank while a model loads and is not empty where WebGL is missing. Check:
the first capture after load, with the network slowed, shows the image.
Wheel and touch scroll the page: the canvas does not capture them unless the person has
clicked or focused it first, and orbit controls never zoom on the wheel by default. Check:
the wheel over the canvas scrolls the page.
The canvas has a name (`aria-label` or a caption) and, when it carries information, the
same information as text beside it.

## Captures for review

Controls frozen and the camera at a fixed position before every capture, as in `browser.md`;
a shot of a model mid-rotation compares nothing. (After img2threejs, Apache-2.0; the cost and
people rules are ours, written for pages rather than models.)
